# M2 Single-player race loop: handoff note

Branch `m2-race-loop`. Built and verified in a GPU-less cloud container (4 cores, 15 GB RAM,
Ubuntu 24.04, Mesa lavapipe) on 2026-10-27. No dependency was added or upgraded.

## What was built

| Item | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| City track data | `data/tracks/downtown-loop/track.toml` | 2.14 km greybox street circuit: 14 corner nodes in driving order, each with corner radius, road width, apron (run-off) per side, elevation, banking and curbs. Eight 90° grid corners, a 470 m main avenue, a fast 18° kink at 150 km/h, a banked 11 m hairpin, a 6 m overpass on a 10 % grade, walls on both sides everywhere (an escape lane at turn 1). Race layout: start line, 8-slot staggered grid, 3 sectors, a checkpoint every 90 m (24), sprint finish. |
| Track compiler | `crates/racer-trackc` (library + `racer-trackc validate/compile`) | Builds the centreline from the nodes (straights joined by circular fillets), samples it every metre with a banked 3D frame, generates road, apron, curb (red/white), wall (inner face, top, outer face down to the ground) and marking meshes, grid poses, checkpoints and greybox buildings. Validation: value ranges, fillets that fit between nodes, inner walls that cannot fold in tight corners, grades ≤ 12 %, banking ≤ 10°, no self-overlap of the corridor, a straight long enough for the grid, sector/start/finish points on the route. `compile` writes `generated/`: the racing line, every car's point-mass model and speed profile, and a summary. |
| Racing line | `racer-trackc/src/line.rs` | Minimum curvature: coordinate descent with a Newton step per offset on the discrete integral of curvature squared (turning angle² / length), inside the corridor the road leaves a car (half width + 0.3 m, wheels may use 0.5 m of curb), from a 32 m to a 1 m sample stride. |
| Car models and speed profiles | `racer-trackc/src/profile.rs` | Point-mass model per car measured on the `racer-dynamics` testbed: sustained lateral grip (skidpad), ABS braking (100–0), drive acceleration per 1 m/s band from a launch-control full-throttle run (shift gaps, TCS, wheel/engine inertia, rolling resistance included), drag and lift from the aero fit. Profile: corner speed from curvature, forward pass with drive inside the traction circle, backward pass with braking inside the friction ellipse, grade included. |
| Race rules | `crates/racer-sim/src/rules.rs` | Plain data, unit tested: progress along the centreline with a sliding search window (continuous, so shortcuts and teleports cannot gain ground), ordered checkpoint gates credited only inside the corridor and taken back when crossed backwards, laps across the line in both directions, lap/sector timing with delta to the best lap, wrong-way detection, reset monitor (flipped, stuck below 1 m/s, off the track: 3 s) with the last valid position, reset placement behind it clear of other cars, standings, sprint goal, DNF timeout. |
| Race loop | `crates/racer-sim/src/race.rs` | `RacePlugin`: countdown with cars held on the brakes (launch control works on the grid), race clock, tracking, resets with 3 s ghosting (solid again only once no car overlaps), the collisions option (full / ghosted first lap / off) through Avian collision layers, standings, finish (lapped cars finish at the line after the winner), DNF 30 s after the winner, results table with gaps, best laps, resets and assists. Circuit, Sprint (the loop opened: grid to a finish just before the grid) and Time Trial (solo). |
| AI drivers | `crates/racer-sim/src/ai.rs` | Drive through `Controls` only, on the same physics and assists as a player. Stanley-style steering at the front axle on the racing line's own heading, curvature feed-forward, yaw-rate damping, a slip-angle limiter (backs the lock off while the fronts slide past their peak). Speed: the driver's own profile (computed at start from the car model at its difficulty's share of the limits) with slope feed-forward and speed feedback, brake pedal mapped through the brake torque, throttle squeezed as the rears near their peak slip, manual gear choice (best-drive gear; an automatic upshifts too early while the throttle comes in). Racecraft: following with a speed cap and time headway, timed overtakes on a free lane beside the car ahead (inside of the next corner first; abandoned if not alongside at the braking point or if the room closes), defending the inside, keeping clear of cars alongside (absolute lateral bounds), spin catching, reversing out of walls, reset requests when recovery fails. Optional mild catch-up (±3 % speed target, never above the car's full-limit profile). |
| Difficulties | `Difficulty::skill` | Novice, Amateur, Pro, Expert, Alien: share of lateral grip / braking / drive used (0.70/0.55/0.80 … 0.995/0.985/1.0), wandering line error (0.45 … 0.02 m), braking-point spread (6 … 0.4 m), mistakes per corner (8 % … 0.2 %: late braking, over-slowing, running wide), overtaking aggression (0.1 … 0.9: how soon it goes for a pass, how close it follows) and contact willingness (0: keeps a wide berth … 1: leans on others and rams back after being hit). |
| Game flow and UI | `crates/racer-game/src/{flow,race_scene,hud,visuals}.rs` | States: main menu → race setup (mode, track, car, AI count 0–7, AI cars same/mixed, difficulty or mixed, laps, collisions, catch-up, ABS/TCS/ESC, steering assist, racing-line assist, gearbox) → race → results → restart or menu, with a pause menu (resume, restart, menu). Keyboard, gamepad and mouse. HUD: position, lap, lap/last/best times, sector times, delta to best, speed, gear, rpm bar, minimap with every car, countdown, wrong-way and off-track warnings. Racing-line assist drawn on the road (green accelerate, red brake). `racer-game race [--car N] [--ai N] [--difficulty D] [--laps N] [--autopilot]` starts a race directly. The M1 driving scene stays as `racer-game drive`. |
| Verification | `racer-capture ai-laps`, `ai-races`, `tools/ai-check.sh`, `tools/plot_race.py` | Headless release-build runs, one simulation per thread (each single-threaded), see below. |
| Captures | `racer-capture scene menu|race` | Menu and setup screens; an 8-car one-lap race with the player on autopilot, fast-forwarded to the grid, the pack, the chase camera with HUD, and the results screen. |
