# M1 Driving feel: handoff note

Branch `m1-driving-feel`. Built and verified in a GPU-less cloud container (4 cores, 15 GB RAM,
Ubuntu 24.04, Mesa lavapipe) on 2026-10-27. Nothing was pinned or upgraded beyond M0 except the
new crates listed under "Dependencies".

## What was built

| Item | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Vehicle model v1 | `crates/racer-dynamics` | Pure Rust (glam, serde, toml; no Bevy). `Vehicle::step(&mut VehicleState, &ChassisState, &Controls, &[Option<WheelContact>; 4], dt) -> StepOutput { force, torque, telemetry }`. `VehicleState` is plain `Copy` data (rollback = copy). 240 Hz chassis step, wheels and drivetrain substepped ×4 inside. |
| Tires | `tire.rs` | Magic Formula per axis with the peak placed at a configured slip (B solved from C, E), combined slip by the similarity method, load-sensitive μ, first-order relaxation (implicit, stable at any speed, a damped spring at standstill), pneumatic-trail aligning torque that fades past the peak, surface grip/drag multipliers (dry/wet asphalt, paint, cobbles, metal, curb, grass, dirt). |
| Suspension | `vehicle.rs` | Spring from ride frequency, bump/rebound damping from damping ratios, progressive bump stop, anti-roll bars, tire forces applied at the axle roll-centre height. Contacts come from the caller's wheel shape cast (distance + normal + surface). |
| Drivetrain | `drivetrain.rs` | Torque curve, closed-throttle drag, idle governor, rev limiter with hysteresis, flywheel inertia, automatic clutch (launch slip, anti-stall, pedal), launch control (brake + full throttle at rest), gearbox with per-gear final drives (DSG), auto and manual shifting, DCT-style shifts (ignition cut / blip with torque fill), open / clutch-type LSD differentials and an AWD centre differential. Every coupling (clutch, LSDs) is solved implicitly per substep: locked couplings are exactly rigid, slipping ones transmit their capacity. |
| Aero | `vehicle.rs` | Drag through the CoM, lift/downforce per axle. |
| Assists | `assists.rs` | ABS and TCS on wheel-speed (kinematic) slip with a proportional slip band and 10 ms actuator lag; TCS trims engine torque, or clutch torque while pulling away; ESC brakes single wheels against a yaw-rate reference capped by grip. Controlled per player through `Controls::assists`. |
| Car data | `data/cars/<id>/car.toml` | Three cars, published values with per-table source URLs, estimates kept apart under `[estimated]`, reference figures under `[reference]` (value, source, note). |
| Headless testbed | `racer-dynamics/src/testbed.rs` | Rigid-body integrator over a height-field ground, analytic sphere-cast contacts, body-box penalty contact, scripted drivers and the reference maneuvers. |
| Avian integration | `crates/racer-sim/src/vehicle.rs` | `VehiclePlugin` (adds `PhysicsPlugins`), `spawn_car` (chassis rigid body with the car's mass and inertia, body collider), per-step cylinder shape casts against world colliders, forces applied with Avian's `Forces` in `FixedUpdate` (240 Hz). |
| Greybox track | `crates/racer-sim/src/track.rs` | ~1.3 km loop: 260 m straight, 15 m hairpin, 30 m left, chicane with curbs, 80 m sweeper, 0.9 m crest on the back straight; skidpad (45.72 m radius circle on a 130 m pad) beside it. The road is one trimesh with internal-edge fixing; curbs, pad and ground are boxes. |
| Test autopilot | `crates/racer-sim/src/autopilot.rs` | Pure pursuit + curvature speed profile, used for telemetry runs only (AI drivers are M2). |
| Driving scene | `crates/racer-game/src/driving.rs` | `racer-game` (or `racer-game drive --car N [--skidpad]`): car selection (1/2/3), track/skidpad (T/K), reset (R), chase camera (smoothed, FOV 60–85° with speed) and hood camera (V), debug HUD (speed, gear, rpm, slip angles and ratios, g, ABS/TCS/ESC). `racer-game greybox` keeps the M0 free-camera scene. |
| Input | `crates/racer-game/src/input.rs` | Keyboard and gamepad (gilrs through Bevy), speed-sensitive steering filter when steering assist is on (lock shrinks with speed, rate-limited; wheels bypass it). `WheelDevice` trait with `NoWheel`, `MockWheel` (tests) and `SdlWheel` (feature `wheel`). Force feedback = front aligning torque through the steering ratio. |
| SDL3 wheel backend | `crates/racer-game/src/sdl_wheel.rs` | SDL3 built from source (static, joystick/haptic/HIDAPI only) on its own thread; constant-force effect on the steering axis. |
| Telemetry runs | `racer-capture telemetry <out-dir> [car...]`, `tools/telemetry.sh` | Headless Bevy + Avian (no renderer): a lap behind the autopilot, a skidpad speed ramp, a launch-control start to 120 km/h and full ABS stop. One CSV per car and maneuver; `tools/plot_telemetry.py` plots them. |
| Driving captures | `racer-capture scene driving`, goldens in `crates/racer-capture/goldens/driving/` | chase, hood, front three-quarter, hairpin, chicane, skidpad. |

### Reference maneuvers

Run headlessly on flat dry asphalt by scripted drivers in `testbed.rs`. Real figures and their
sources are in each `car.toml`. Tolerances:

| Maneuver | Tolerance | Procedure |
|---|---|---|
| 0–100 km/h | ±8 % | Launch control (1.5 s brake + full throttle), automatic, ABS/TCS/ESC on, timed from brake release |
| 100–0 km/h | ±8 % | Cruise at 100 km/h, then full brake with ABS, distance until stopped |
| Skidpad | ±0.08 g | 45.72 m radius (300 ft pad), assists off, speed raised 0.08 m/s per s; best 1 s average of v²/r while within 0.75 m of the line |
| Top speed | ±4 % | Full throttle until no gain for 10 s |
| Step steer | stable | 100 km/h, step to the steering for 0.4 g on a neutral car, with and without ESC: sideslip < 3°, yaw-rate overshoot < 30 %, yaw rate steady (variation < 0.01 rad/s over the last second), lateral 0.3–0.5 g; plus a 4× step with ESC: sideslip < 10° |

| Car | 0–100 sim / real (s) | 100–0 sim / real (m) | Skidpad sim / real (g) | Top speed sim / real (km/h) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Golf GTI | 5.94 / 6.3 (−5.7 %) | 36.34 / 36.4 (−0.2 %) | 0.96 / 0.97 | 249.98 / 250 (governed) |
| M3 Competition | 3.95 / 3.9 (+1.3 %) | 35.78 / 36.0 (−0.6 %) | 1.02 / 1.03 | 250.03 / 250 (governed) |
| 911 Carrera S | 3.64 / 3.7 (−1.6 %) | 31.11 / 31.4 (−0.9 %) | 1.06 / 1.06 | 313.06 / 308 (+1.6 %) |

Real 0–100 times are manufacturer claims. Braking figures are magazine 70–0 / 60–0 mph tests
scaled by v² (no 100–0 km/h magazine figures were found); the notes in each file say which.
The GTI's measured 0–100 in an instrumented test was 5.88 s, so the simulated 5.94 s is closer to
that than to VW's 6.3 s claim. Top speed for the GTI and M3 is the electronic limiter (the test
checks that the car reaches it); the 911's is drag-limited.

Step steer at 100 km/h (yaw rate rad/s, sideslip max):

| Car | 0.4 g step, ESC off | 0.4 g step, ESC on | 4× step, ESC on |
|---|---|---|---|
| Golf GTI | 0.116 steady, 0.35 g, 0.1° | same | 0.353 steady, 1.00 g, 1.2° |
| M3 | 0.126, 0.38 g, 0.2° | same | 0.373, 1.06 g, 1.5° |
| 911 | 0.124, 0.37 g, 0.3° | same | 0.427, 1.16 g, 3.2° |

(The lateral g above is the accelerometer reading, which includes a few hundredths of g from body roll.)

| Launch rpm | 3000 | 3500 | 4500 |
| Differential | front LSD 40 Nm + 0.3/0.1 | rear LSD 80 Nm + 0.5/0.3 | rear LSD 50 Nm + 0.3/0.5 |
| Frontal area | 2.22 m² (from Golf 8 CdA) | 2.34 m² (published) | 2.20 m² |

Torque curves are shaped to the published torque plateau and power band (the power at the
published rpm matches to within 1 %); idle, engine inertia and drag are class norms.

Fits worth knowing about: the M3 needs more rear than front longitudinal grip (wider, driven 285
rears; with 47 % rear weight it is purely traction-limited off the line) and a rear-light brake
balance (about 80/20) to match a 36 m stop that is long for its tires. Everything else uses the
same tire shape for all cars and differs only in μ.

### Telemetry review

```
$ tools/telemetry.sh captures/telemetry      # 9 CSV + 9 PNG, ~1 min after a release build
```

Plots for all three cars are in `docs/milestones/m1/`. What they show, after the fixes listed
further down:

- **Balance (skidpad, front minus rear slip angle against lateral g):** GTI understeers and
  increasingly so (0.16° at 0.5 g, 0.72° at 0.9 g); M3 mildly (0.16°, 0.52°); 911 close to
  neutral (0.06°, 0.16°) with body sideslip turning tail-out near the limit. That ordering is
  what the layouts and weight distributions predict.
- **Weight transfer:** outside wheels load up in corners (inside rear of the GTI drops below 1 kN
  at 0.95 g, the classic FWD three-wheel stance); front loads rise and rears drop under braking
  (front axle static to braking: GTI 9.2 → 12.0 kN, M3 9.4 → 12.3 kN, 911 5.6 → 8.6 kN), the
  reverse under acceleration; the 911 carries most
  of its load on the rear throughout.
- **Gear changes:** launch control holds launch rpm, then clean upshifts near the limiter with a
  short rpm drop each (DCT fill), and downshifts under braking; the governed cars sit on 250 km/h.
- **Slip:** a short wheelspin peak at launch trimmed by TCS to about 1.2× peak slip; ABS holds
  braking slip near the peak to a standstill; lap slip angles stay within 3° except at the crest
  landing.
- **Crest:** at ~100 km/h all cars take off (~0.5 s) and land with 30–65 kN peak wheel loads and
  a short rebound, then carry on (the lateral-g spikes in the lap plots are the landing).

### Input, wheel and force feedback

`InputPlugin` takes the newest device: wheel if one reports, otherwise gamepad when its stick or
triggers move, otherwise keyboard. Tests: steering filter (lock shrinks with speed with assist on,
rate limit, wheels unfiltered), FFB sign/saturation, and a full app test where `MockWheel` drives
the car through `InputPlugin` + `VehiclePlugin` and receives force feedback every step (pulling
the rim back towards centre while turning). SDL3:

```
$ cargo test -p racer-game --features wheel     # builds SDL3 from source (cmake), ~1.5 min
test sdl_wheel::tests::initialises_without_a_device ... ok
$ VK_DRIVER_FILES=... xvfb-run target/debug/racer-game drive --car 3   # with --features wheel
                                                 # 60 s, no warnings or errors
```

No wheel exists in the container, so reading axes and playing the constant-force effect on real
hardware is untested. The axis mapping is Logitech-style (axis 0 steer, 1–3 pedals, buttons 4/5
paddles). The feature is off by default, so builds on any OS need neither cmake nor a C
compiler.

## Problems found while building, and fixes

1. **ABS limit cycles.** An integral ABS controller on the tire's relaxed slip oscillated
   between locking and full release; replaced by a proportional slip band with actuator lag, and
   later switched to kinematic (wheel-speed sensor) slip because the relaxed slip lags more and
   more as speed drops.
2. **Launch bog.** Dumping the clutch at the launch speed spun the wheels and dragged the engine
   to idle. The clutch now carries about the engine's torque while slipping, TCS trims the clutch
   instead of the engine while pulling away, and launch control holds the launch speed.
3. **Ghost collisions.** Built from 2 m boxes, the road made the chassis collider catch box end
   faces at speed (a ~600 g stop after the crest landing). The road is now one trimesh with
   internal-edge fixing.
4. **Noisy contact normals.** Parry's cylinder cast returned normals tilted by up to 13° on flat
   boxes, buzzing the suspension. The cast still finds the contact; a short ray at the contact
   point gives the normal, and the distance is solved exactly against that plane.
5. **Accelerometer blips.** Single-step solver blips (~2 mm/s, invisible) showed as 20 % spikes
   in velocity-difference acceleration; the logged acceleration is low-passed (20 ms).

## Known gaps

- **Camber** (thrust and camber/toe curves vs travel), **ride-height-sensitive aero**, active
  aero, slipstream and torque vectoring are not modelled. The tire has no temperature or wear
  (out of scope per the plan). Wheels have no unsprung mass (quasi-static vertical).
- **Steering-wheel hardware** is untested (no device); the SDL path builds, initialises and is
  covered through the trait and mock only.
- **Quarter-mile test** from PLAN.md section 4 is not implemented (not in the M1 task list).
- **Reference figures:** braking distances are converted from mph tests; the M3 weight
  distribution and GTI limiter are estimates. The M3 front/rear grip split and brake balance are
  fits, not measurements.
- The **autopilot** is a test driver (a little brake chatter and steering dither at the hairpin);
  real AI drivers are M2.
- **Telemetry and captures** are not run by `cargo test` (they need a release build / Xvfb);
  `tools/telemetry.sh` and `tools/capture.sh` are the entry points, and the CI capture job runs
  both. CI itself still cannot start jobs on this account (see M0), so the first green CI run
  is still owed.
- `tools/plot_telemetry.py` needs a Python that can import the distro's matplotlib; this
  container's default `python3` is 3.13 while Ubuntu's package targets 3.12, so
  `tools/telemetry.sh` picks whichever works.
